SimpaQr: Scanner & Studio

SimpaQr is an ultra-clean, Apple card-inspired barcode and QR intelligence suite built with Jetpack Compose. All standard QR scanning, creation, and decoding are processed 100% locally on-device without cloud dependency.

Key Pillars

  • Local Privacy: Zero data harvesting. Barcodes are decoded directly on your processor.
  • UPI & PhonePe Integration: 1-Tap payment launches for PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, and BHIM.
  • 10+ Studio Tools: AI visual analyzer, document inspector, Wi-Fi EasyConnect, vCard, secret locker, and batch scanner.
  • Threat Radar: On-device link risk analysis identifying phishing patterns, IP hosts, and malicious domains.
  • AI Vision Intelligence: Powered by Gemini 3.1 Pro with High Thinking mode for deep document and visual inspection.
