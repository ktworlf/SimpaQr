Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

SimpaQr values your digital privacy. This document outlines how data is handled within the application.

1. On-Device Local Processing

SimpaQr performs camera frame decoding, barcode reading, and QR code generation directly within your device's memory. No video streams or camera frames are uploaded to third-party servers.

2. Camera & Photo Permissions

Camera access is exclusively utilized in real time to locate barcodes in the viewfinder. Photo library access is strictly scoped to images you explicitly select.

3. Cloud Synchronization (Optional)

If you choose to authenticate via Google or Firebase Auth, your scan history is securely synchronized to your private Firestore database for multi-device access.

4. AI Features (Gemini)

When you explicitly trigger AI Visual Analysis, Document AI, or chat with the Simpa Guide, the selected image or question is sent securely to Google's Gemini API for processing and returned to your screen.

5. Contact Us

For questions or privacy inquiries: privacy@simpaqr.app
