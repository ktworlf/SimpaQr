AuraQR: Scanner & Studio

AuraQR is an iOS-inspired, privacy-first barcode & QR intelligence suite built with Jetpack Compose. All standard QR scanning, creation, and decoding are processed 100% locally on-device without cloud dependency.

Key Pillars

  • Local Privacy: Zero data harvesting. Barcodes are decoded directly on your processor.
  • Smart Studio: Create custom iOS-styled QR codes for Wi-Fi networks, vCards, phone numbers, and web links.
  • Threat Radar: On-device link risk analysis identifying phishing patterns, IP hosts, and malicious domains.
  • AI Vision Intelligence: Powered by Gemini 3.1 Pro with High Thinking mode for deep document and visual inspection.
  • Cloud Backup: Optional seamless Firestore sync with Google Sign-in to sync your scan archive across devices.
